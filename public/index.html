How it Works

1
Create an Account
Sign up and verify your identity with Aadhaar to build trust in your reports and unlock full participation.
2
Report a Problem
Spot a broken road, waterlogging, power outage or any civic issue? Post it with a photo, description and your location in seconds.
3
Community Upvotes
Neighbours upvote issues they care about. High-upvote issues surface to the top and get prioritised by local authorities.
4
Authority Action
Verified local government officials and municipal bodies review, accept and post updates directly on the platform.
5
Issue Resolved
Once fixed, the issue is marked resolved. Citizens can confirm and rate the resolution. Earn badges for your contributions.

Why JanAwaaz?

India's civic problems are visible to everyone but acted on by few. JanAwaaz bridges the gap between citizens and local government by creating a transparent, accountable, and data-driven platform for civic action.

🔍
Full Transparency
Every issue, update and resolution is publicly visible. No more issues disappearing into a complaint box with no follow-up.
🛡️
Aadhaar-Verified Trust
Optional Aadhaar verification prevents spam and fake reports, ensuring authorities receive credible, actionable complaints.
📍
Hyperlocal & Nationwide
Browse issues by your district, or zoom out to see patterns across all of India on the national map view.
🏅
Gamified Civic Engagement
Earn points and badges for reporting, verifying and following up on issues. Top contributors are recognised on the leaderboard.
